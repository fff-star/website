Understanding Zero-Knowledge Proofs

How to prove you know something without revealing what you know.

May 28, 2025

A zero-knowledge proof is a method by which one party can prove to another that a statement is true without revealing any information beyond the validity of the statement itself.

The concept was introduced by Goldwasser, Micali, and Rackoff in 1985. It has since become a foundational primitive in modern cryptography, enabling applications from authentication to blockchain privacy...
