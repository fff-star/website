🎨 Visual Style Direction

Current: "Arctic Frost" — cold, icy whites & slate blues. Which way?

A

Keep Cold, Refine It

Same blue-slate palette, but add depth — subtle gradients, layered shadows, better spacing rhythm, more refined typography scale. Evolution, not revolution.

B

Warm It Up

Introduce amber/gold accents, warm grays, cream tones. Keep the clean aesthetic but give it personality and warmth. Think: a well-lit study, not a laboratory.

C

High-Contrast Minimal

Sharper black/white contrast, more whitespace, geometric precision. Strip decoration, let content breathe. Monochrome with one bold accent.
